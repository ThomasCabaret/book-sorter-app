BookDuels recommendation model notice
=====================================

The bundled recommendation asset is a transformed model derived from
goodbooks-10k v1.0 by Zygmunt Zajac and contributors:
https://github.com/zygmuntz/goodbooks-10k

Source dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The asset contains frozen item factors and bibliographic metadata. It does not
contain BookDuels votes, raw goodbooks rating rows, or identifiable reader
profiles. Personalization is computed locally from the app's active vote log.

This statistical model reflects the limitations and selection biases of its
source ratings. Its scores are not calibrated probabilities or guarantees that
a reader will like a book.
